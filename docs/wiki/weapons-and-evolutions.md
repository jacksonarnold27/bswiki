# Weapons and evolutions

## How a Bonkscape weapon works

Every OSRS weapon takes over one Megabonk weapon (its *Megabonk base*). It keeps that weapon's **behaviour**: how it
attacks, its projectile and its visuals. Bonkscape replaces the rest:

- **Base stats**: damage, cooldown, area, amount (projectiles), speed (projectile speed), bounces, duration and
  knockback come from the row below.
- **Level-up upgrades**: the weapon's Megabonk upgrade pool is replaced with the row's list.
- **Name and icon**: the OSRS name, and an icon rendered from your cache. The four Strikes, Ice Barrage and the Fire
  Surge evolution use their OSRS spell icons; the rest use the item's inventory icon.

On Bonkscape's maps, level-ups offer only Bonkscape's weapons: one for each of twelve Megabonk weapons (a ladder's
at your tier), plus a Barrows brother's own weapon to him alone (Guthan's warspear, Torag's hammers and Verac's flail
share the Void knight mace's Megabonk weapon, so they replace the mace while their brother plays), and only those whose
[requirements](#requirements) you meet. They all count as unlocked whatever your Megabonk progress is. When a weapon fires:

- its projectile carries the OSRS model from the sheet, about a quarter of a tile along its longest side, centred
  on the projectile and turned to point along its flight: each shortbow's arrow (iron for the
  Shortbow up to rune for the yew and magic shortbows), the Toxic blowpipe's dragon dart, the Red chinchompa, Karil's crossbow's bolt, and the Strikes', Ice Barrage's, the Trident's and Ahrim's
  staff's spell graphics (the Abyssal whip, Dragon scimitar, Scythe of vitur, Armadyl godsword, Dragon claws, Void
  knight mace and the other Barrows weapons have none). Megabonk's own projectile effects stay;
- the weapon's OSRS attack sound plays, at most every 0.3 s;
- your adventurer takes that weapon in hand and plays its OSRS attack animation ([Adventurers](adventurers.md#looks)).

## Weapon ladders

Three of the weapons are **ladders** of OSRS tiers on one Megabonk weapon. The Warrior, Ranger, Mage, Ironman and Pure
start each run on the highest tier their own levels allow ([Adventurers](adventurers.md#starting-weapons)), and a
level-up offers the ladder at your tier:

| Ladder | Tiers, with the level each is climbed at |
|---|---|
| Scimitars (Attack) | Bronze 1, iron 2, steel 5, black 10, mithril 20, adamant 30, rune 40, dragon 60 |
| Shortbows (Ranged) | Shortbow 1, oak 5, willow 20, maple 30, yew 40, magic 50 |
| Strikes (Magic) | Wind 1, Water 5, Earth 9, Fire 13 |

Iron asks Attack 1 in OSRS, as bronze does, so a new adventurer starts with bronze and climbs to iron at Attack 2. Each
tier has its own OSRS model and damage, from 0.70 of the top tier's at the bottom, and keeps its ladder's special
attack (the scimitars' Sever, the shortbows' Snapshot; each Strike its own: Gale, Torrent, Quake and Wildfire). A tier
reached during a run comes with the next one: *"You can now wield the rune scimitar (from your next run)."* The
Zerker keeps his own dragon scimitar whatever his Attack.

## Requirements

Every weapon asks its OSRS levels: the ladders' tiers as above, the Abyssal whip Attack 70, the Armadyl godsword 75,
Dragon claws 60, the Scythe of vitur Attack 80 and Strength 90, the Toxic blowpipe Ranged 75, the Red chinchompa
Ranged 55, the Trident of the seas Magic 75, Ice Barrage Magic 94, the Void knight mace 42 in every combat skill and
Prayer 22. A level-up never offers a weapon above your levels; one you hold already, start with or carry as an extra
weapon (Durial321's Ice Barrage) is exempt. When a level brings one into reach, the chat box names it: *"You can now
wield the abyssal whip."*

## Special attacks

Nine of the weapons have a special attack, counting each ladder once (every tier of a ladder has it). They share one
**special attack energy** bar, the special attack orb beside the minimap ([HUD](hud-and-chat.md#in-a-run)):

- It starts each run full (100%) and refills completely in 60 seconds. Each kill adds 0.25% more, but kills can at
  most double the refill rate.
- **F** (`Special.FireKey`; **Y** on a controller) fires the **armed** weapon's spec at the nearest monster in reach: 5 tiles for melee
  weapons, 12 for ranged and magic, and no farther than its own radius for a spec centred on you (Wildfire, Deep
  Freeze). With nothing in reach or too little energy nothing is spent, and the chat says *There's nothing in reach.*
  or *You don't have enough special attack energy left.*
- **Q** (`Special.SwitchKey`; a left-stick click on a controller) arms the next weapon you hold that has a spec; the chat names it, as in *Special attack:
  Abyssal whip (Energy Drain).* The armed weapon's icon sits beside the orb, which lights up when that spec can fire.
- A spec plays your adventurer's OSRS special attack animation and its OSRS graphic, then hits through Megabonk's own
  weapon damage, so hitsplats, XP and kills work as for any hit. A spec hit is a multiple of the weapon's average hit
  over its last 20 hits (before its first hit, its damage stat). It hits only monsters that can take damage: Megabonk
  ignores every hit on a monster for about its first second.

| Weapon | Special attack | Energy | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Abyssal whip | Energy Drain | 50% | Hits for 1.25x, and slows the target and every monster within 2 tiles of it to half speed for 4 s |
| Scimitars (every tier) | Sever | 55% | Hits for 1.5x and stuns the target for 1.5 s |
| Shortbows (every tier) | Snapshot | 55% | Two hits of 1.1x, 0.2 s apart |
| Toxic blowpipe | Toxic Siphon | 50% | Hits for 1.5x and heals you half the damage it dealt |
| Strikes | Gale (Wind), Torrent (Water), Quake (Earth), Wildfire (Fire): Bonkscape's own | 50% | Hits every monster in a 70 degree cone 5 tiles long for 1.5x |
| Ice Barrage | Deep Freeze (Bonkscape's own) | 50% | Hits every monster within 4 tiles of you for 0.5x and freezes it for 3 s; its ice block shows on the target |
| Trident of the seas | Tidal Chain (Bonkscape's own) | 50% | Hits 5 monsters in a chain, each the nearest within 3 tiles of the last, for 1x, 0.12 s apart |
| Armadyl godsword | The Judgement | 50% | Hits for 2.5x, and every other monster within 2 tiles of the target for 0.5x |
| Dragon claws | Slice and Dice | 50% | Four hits of 1.2x, 0.6x, 0.4x and 0.4x, 0.12 s apart |

The Red chinchompa, the Scythe of vitur, the Void knight mace and the Barrows brothers' weapons have none. The
Strikes, Ice Barrage and the Trident have no special attack in OSRS, nor do the ladders' lower tiers (only the dragon
scimitar and the magic shortbow); Bonkscape gives them one.

## Caskets

Megabonk's chests wear the OSRS **Casket** model, and their map icon stays. Opening one gives Megabonk's normal chest
reward. Opening a casket is also when evolutions happen.

## Evolutions

An evolution turns a maxed weapon into its OSRS upgrade. When you open a casket, Bonkscape checks every evolution:

1. You have its weapon at **level 8 or higher**, and
2. you hold its **gear passive** (any level).

If both are true, the weapon evolves on the spot: its base stats are multiplied as below, it takes the new name and
icon, the chat box says *"Your Abyssal whip has become an Abyssal tentacle!"*, a dialogue in the chat box shows the new
name, and
OSRS's level-up sound plays. Each evolution happens once per run, and every run starts with the plain weapons again.

open a casket.
